Auto Repair Shop Template

Reusable site template for auto repair shops — services, recommended parts, maintenance tips, gallery, and pickup/tow-truck info, all editable from a custom admin panel. Built with .NET 9, Vue 3, and MySQL.

Goal: Give any auto repair shop a professional site — services, parts, and gallery — ready to launch and manage without touching code.

Photo gallery

Services with free-text pricing or discounted combo packages

Recommended parts with a "where to buy" link
